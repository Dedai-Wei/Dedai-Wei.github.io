About

I am a second-year master's student at the University of Macau and an AI for Science researcher developing computational methods for complex scientific and engineering problems. My work combines mathematical modeling, stochastic optimization, and modern deep learning, with particular interests in neural networks, probabilistic diffusion models, scientific machine learning, and large language model agents. I have published nine peer-reviewed papers and am preparing for PhD applications for Fall 2027.

Research Trajectory

My research is grounded in mathematics and statistics. Since 2024, I have progressed from stochastic processes, analytical derivation, mathematical modeling, and optimization to scientific prediction and modern learning systems. My current work integrates graph diffusion, generative models, reinforcement learning, large language models, and autonomous agents to build reliable AI systems for complex scientific and engineering problems.
