About

PhD applications · Fall 2027

I work with Dr. Sin Chi Kuok at the UM State Key Laboratory of IoT for Smart City. My research draws on stochastic processes, statistical inference, and mathematical optimization to develop graph learning, probabilistic diffusion models, and physics-informed neural operators for AI for Science. I focus on learning across different physical regimes and quantifying uncertainty, while exploring large language models (LLMs) and agent systems for scientific analysis and discovery.
