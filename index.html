About

I am a Research Assistant at the University of Macau's State Key Laboratory of Internet of Things for Smart City, working with Dr. Sin Chi Kuok. My research integrates stochastic processes, statistical inference, mathematical modeling, and optimization with machine learning to develop reliable computational and generative methods for scientific and engineering problems, with a focus on uncertainty quantification, physical constraints, and generalization. My work has appeared in Pattern Recognition, Neural Networks, Journal of Advanced Research, Results in Engineering, and Biomedical Signal Processing and Control. I am preparing for PhD applications for Fall 2027.

Current focus

I study text-enhanced dynamic graph diffusion for structural-change discovery (Information Sciences, under review); physics-informed neural operators through PIC-GO (AAAI 2027, CCF A, under review); and engineering-constrained scenario generation through StressGDiff (Reliability Engineering & System Safety, under review). EI-HLRD explores probabilistic ground-motion modeling and cross-regional transfer, while FADC and CausalMB-LLM investigate natural-language-guided discovery and causal structure learning.

My generative-agent study, Insurance as AI Risk Infrastructure, was accepted at an AAMAS workshop; its extended version is under review at IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence (CiteScore 6.4). See projects, publication details, and venue metrics →
